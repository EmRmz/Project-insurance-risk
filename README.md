# Project-insurance-risk
# Automotive Insurance Risk and Transition Modeling

Project developed for the **Modeling and Simulation** course. This project applies concepts of Markov chains and transition matrices to model how the risk profile of insured drivers evolves over time.

## Project Description
Insurance companies need to predict the probability that a customer will change risk categories (e.g., moving from a safe-driver bonus tier to higher rates due to accumulated claims). 

This model simulates the annual transitions of policyholders between different risk states using historical data and linear algebra.

## Objectives
* **Define system states:** Establish risk categories based on claim history (e.g., *Preferential, Standard, Moderate Risk, High Risk*).
* **Build the transition matrix:** Calculate empirical probabilities of state changes using an insurance claims dataset.
* **Simulate scenarios:** Project the behavior of the customer portfolio over 5 and 10 years (stationary distribution) and evaluate the impact of changes in insurance company policies.
